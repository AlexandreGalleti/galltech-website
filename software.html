Software FAQ

Yes. Code, database, and hosting accounts are yours. If we ever part ways, you keep everything and any developer can take over.

Usually, yes — most modern tools have APIs or export options. Connecting existing tools is often cheaper than replacing them, and we'll tell you when that's the case.

Every system is quoted by scope after we map your workflow. You always get the full price in writing before anything is built — no hourly surprises.
