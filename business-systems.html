We're small — do we really need an ERP or CRM?

Maybe not yet, and we'll say so. Many clients start with a well-organized shared drive, a simple CRM, and solid backup — then grow into bigger systems when the volume justifies it.

It depends on your internet reliability, data size, and workflow. Often the answer is hybrid: cloud for email and files, local for cameras and heavy data. We quote both honestly.
